### **Pillar 1: AI for Students \- Nurturing the AI-Literate Generation**

* **Student AI Literacy Programme:** Age-appropriate curriculum Cl.3-12 across 4 stages: Foundational \-\> Intermediate \-\> Advanced \-\> Expert. 5 Big Ideas in AI \+ Indian context.  
* **Student AI Innovation Labs:** Designated school spaces for prototyping AI solutions. Monthly challenge calendar, EdTech toolkits, and annual SAIL Showcase.  
* **IPN AI Olympiad:** India's first school network AI competition. 3 rounds: School \-\> Regional \-\> National. AI Ideathon, Responsible AI Essay, AI for Social Good.  
* **Student AI Clubs & Peer Networks:** Each affiliated school establishes a Student AI Club (min. 20 members, Cl. 6+) with elected AI Ambassadors connecting across IPN's national peer learning network.

### **Pillar 2: AI for Teachers \- Empowering the Architects of Learning**

**IPN Teacher AI Bootcamp (3-Day Intensive)**

* Track 1: Foundations (AI basics & classroom integration)  
* Track 2: Practice (Subject-wise AI integration)  
* Track 3: Ethics & Safety (Data privacy & academic integrity)  
* Track 4: Leadership (For HoDs, Coordinators & Senior Teachers)  
* **AI Mentor Teacher Network (AMTN):** 500 Mentor Teachers trained, recognised with IPN AI Fellowship & resource portfolio to cascade learning.  
* **AI Classroom Certification (IPN-AICC):** Two-level certification: AI-Aware Classroom \-\> AI-Integrated Classroom, with peer-observed evidence portfolio.  
* **AI Instructional Design Studio:** Cloud platform: AI lesson planner, multilingual (10+ languages), peer resource sharing.

### **Pillar 3: AI for School Leaders \- Governing the Future-Ready School**

* **AI Manthan Conference Series:** India's premier school leadership conference on AI \- multi-city, annual, with keynotes, workshops, case studies & Action Plan sessions.  
* **IPN AI Leadership Cert. (AiLCP):** 6-month blended programme, 6 modules: AI Landscape \-\> Strategy \-\> Ethics \-\> Instructional Leadership \-\> Operations \-\> Capstone.  
* **AI School Governance Framework:** Ready-to-adopt policy toolkit: Acceptable Use Policy, DPDP Act compliance, Academic Integrity Guidelines, Procurement Checklist.  
* **India School AI Readiness Index:** Annual benchmarking across 5 dimensions: Infrastructure, Curriculum, Teacher Capacity, Leadership, Ethics. National ISAIRI Dashboard.  
* **IPN Certified AI School Leader (CASL):** India's FIRST leadership-grade AI certification for school principals and education leaders.
