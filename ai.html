The IPN FORUM AI Ecosystem: Three Pillars

A unified national programme for students, teachers, and school leaders.

Pillar 1

AI for Students

Nurturing the AI-Literate Generation
Target: 5,00,000+ Students by 2028
  • Student AI Literacy Programme: Age-appropriate curriculum Cl.3–12 across 4 stages.
  • Student AI Innovation Labs: Designated school spaces for prototyping.
  • IPN AI Olympiad: India's first school network AI competition.
  • Student AI Clubs: Peer networks with elected AI Ambassadors.
Pillar 2

AI for Teachers

Empowering the Architects of Learning
Target: 1,00,000+ Teachers by 2028
  • IPN Teacher AI Bootcamp: 3-Day intensive covering foundations, practice, ethics, and leadership.
  • AI Mentor Teacher Network: 500 Mentor Teachers deployed nationwide.
  • AI Classroom Certification (IPN-AICC): Peer-observed evidence portfolio.
  • AI Instructional Design Studio: Cloud platform for lesson planning.
Pillar 3

AI for School Leaders

Governing the Future-Ready School
Target: 5,000+ Leaders by 2028
  • AI Manthan Conference Series: Premier multi-city leadership conference.
  • IPN AI Leadership Cert. (AiLCP): 6-month blended strategic programme.
  • AI School Governance Framework: Ready-to-adopt policy toolkit.
  • IPN Certified AI School Leader (CASL): India's FIRST leadership-grade AI certification.
